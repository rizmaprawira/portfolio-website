About Me
Hi, I'm Rizma Prawira, a Meteorology graduate from Institut Teknologi Bandung (ITB), Indonesia, with a background in atmospheric and climate research, scientific data analysis, and education.
My academic and research experience focuses on understanding atmospheric processes, climate variability, and their impacts on society. I've worked on projects involving ENSO teleconnections, tropical cyclones, climate change, and climate-related risk, combining observational datasets, statistical analysis, and numerical modeling to investigate real-world problems.
Beyond research, I enjoy exploring how technology can make information more accessible and useful. My interests extend to Python programming, data visualization, automation, and the development of practical tools for scientific and everyday applications.
Teaching is another important part of my journey. Through university teaching assistantships, practical sessions, and private tutoring, I've helped students navigate topics ranging from statistics and programming to atmospheric science and mathematics.
I created this website to document my work, share code and educational resources, and write about the things I'm learning and exploring—both within and beyond atmospheric science.

03 / RESEARCH & PUBLICATIONS
Research overview
My research interests lie at the intersection of tropical meteorology, climate variability, atmospheric dynamics, and applied environmental science.
I am particularly interested in understanding how large-scale climate phenomena influence regional weather and rainfall patterns, especially across the Maritime Continent. My work combines observational and reanalysis datasets, statistical methods, atmospheric diagnostics, and numerical modeling to investigate these relationships.
Beyond fundamental climate research, I have also contributed to projects involving extreme rainfall, climate-risk assessment, parametric insurance, and atmospheric water harvesting. These experiences have strengthened my interest in translating scientific understanding into practical applications that support environmental resilience and decision-making.
Featured research projects
Changing ENSO–Rainfall Teleconnections over the Maritime Continent
Undergraduate thesis · ITB · 2026
Investigated changes in the relationship between El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO) and boreal-winter rainfall across the Maritime Continent during 1981–2025. Combined rainfall analysis, atmospheric circulation diagnostics, moisture convergence, and idealized numerical experiments to examine the mechanisms behind evolving regional rainfall responses.
Keywords: Climate Variability · ENSO · Atmospheric Dynamics · Linear Baroclinic Model
Synoptic Drivers of Tropical Cyclone Senyar
Research Assistant · ITB · 2026
Contributed to a collaborative investigation of the atmospheric conditions associated with Tropical Cyclone Senyar and longer-term changes in near-equatorial tropical cyclone activity over the Maritime Continent. Supported meteorological data processing, scientific visualization, and interpretation.
Keywords: Tropical Cyclones · Synoptic Meteorology · Climate Variability
ENSO-Related Atmospheric Variability over Papua Island
Research Internship · BRIN · Jun–Aug 2025
Examined ENSO-related atmospheric variability over Papua Island using climatological wind datasets and meteorological analysis. Processed and visualized atmospheric data to explore regional circulation patterns and their relationship with large-scale climate variability.

Climate Change & Extreme Precipitation Assessment

05 / CODE, DATA & AI
Code, Data & AI overview
Human curiosity. AI-accelerated execution.
I use artificial intelligence extensively to turn ideas into practical tools, automate repetitive tasks, and solve data-related problems more efficiently.
My approach to programming is fundamentally AI-assisted. Rather than developing every line of code independently, I rely heavily on AI coding assistants to generate, structure, debug, and refine code. This allows me to focus on defining problems, designing workflows, applying domain knowledge, and evaluating results.
My background in atmospheric science, statistics, and data analysis helps me understand what a solution needs to accomplish and assess whether its outputs are scientifically or logically sound.
This section documents the datasets, scripts, applications, and automation workflows I've developed with AI assistance. I share them alongside their sources, methodologies, limitations, and documentation to make the work as transparent and reproducible as possible.
I see AI not simply as a productivity tool, but as an essential part of how I work, learn, and build.
How I Build With AI
I don't code entirely on my own—and I don't pretend to.
AI coding assistants are central to my development process. I depend on them extensively for implementation, syntax, debugging, refactoring, and exploring unfamiliar technologies.
Define the problem: Identify objectives, requirements, constraints, and expected outcomes.
Design the workflow: Determine how data, processes, and components should work together.
Direct AI implementation: Use iterative prompting and feedback to generate and improve code.
Evaluate outputs: Inspect results, troubleshoot issues, and test whether solutions meet their intended purpose.
Apply domain expertise: Use my background in atmospheric science and quantitative analysis to interpret results and recognize potential errors.
I am more confident in applying, adapting, and evaluating code than writing complex software independently from scratch.
My strength is using AI-assisted development to bridge the gap between an idea and a usable solution—not claiming to be a conventional software engineer.

Indonesian Postal Code Dataset & Crawler
PUBLIC REPOSITORY · Dataset · Web Scraping · Python · CSV/XLSX
A Python-based data collection workflow for retrieving Indonesian postal-code information from the official Pos Indonesia website. The project transforms extracted records into structured CSV and Excel datasets, implements validation checks, and documents data provenance.
