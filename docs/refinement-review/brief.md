## 2. UPDATED COLOR SYSTEM — MANDATORY

The current pale blue and pink accents are too weak.

Replace the washed-out palette with a more confident, saturated system.

Primary colors:

- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Near-black: `#181818`
- Strong blue: `#2458D3`
- Strong red: `#DE3D4B`

Secondary colors:

- Light neutral: `#F5F6F7`
- Light blue tint: `#E8F0FF`
- Light red tint: `#FCEAEC`

Use these as an initial design system, adjusting only when necessary for contrast and visual coherence.

### Color hierarchy

- White should remain the dominant background.
- Black anchors typography and high-contrast elements.
- Blue identifies scientific research, data, or technical content.
- Red provides emphasis for teaching, educational content, and selected highlights.
- Secondary tints provide breathing room, not the primary visual identity.

I want blue and red to be immediately recognizable.

Do not use them only in tiny badges or pale icon backgrounds.

At least some major design elements should feature real, saturated color.

Examples:
- A bold blue research section header.
- A prominent red teaching highlight.
- Large category icons with strong colors.
- Colored geometric accents supporting actual content.
- Selected buttons, links, or navigation states.

Use color intentionally, not uniformly. Do not alternate every section between blue and red.

Do not introduce extra decorative colors without a clear reason.

Maintain WCAG AA text contrast.

**I want bold colors, not flashy colors.**
